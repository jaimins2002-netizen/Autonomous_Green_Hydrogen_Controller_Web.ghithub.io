Milestone 2
Fuzzy controller

A Mamdani fuzzy-logic implementation that translates renewable power, water flow, stack temperature, and tank pressure into a hydrogen production command.

Hydrogen-tank pressure0–100 barLow · Medium · High
Hydrogen production rate0–10 kg/hOff · Low · Medium · High

Implementation approach

The controller uses triangular membership functions, an expert Mamdani rule base, and defuzzification to produce a continuous command.

What this milestone establishes

The supplied source specification defines the triangular membership points and the 11 expert rules used by later implementation and validation phases.

It also establishes the safety direction: production should be reduced as hydrogen-tank pressure approaches the high-pressure region.

Educational scope. The specification is a simulation foundation. It is not a certified process-safety system and must not be connected to real hydrogen-production equipment without qualified engineering review and independent safeguards.
